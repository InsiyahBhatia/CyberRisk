# Acme Corp Vendor Security Policy (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT)

> Synthetic document created for the CyberRisk demo. Acme Corp is fictional. Not a real policy.

Framework: Internal
Version: 1.2
Source type: synthetic internal policy

## 1. Scope

Applies to every third party that processes, stores or has access to Acme Corp data or systems.

## 2. Tiering

Vendors are tiered by criticality and data access. CRITICAL vendors support essential business services or handle Restricted data. HIGH vendors handle Confidential data. MEDIUM and LOW vendors have limited or no data access.

## 3. Assessment requirements

CRITICAL and HIGH vendors must complete a security assessment before onboarding and annually thereafter. The assessment reviews the vendor's independent audit report (such as SOC 2 Type II), MFA enforcement, encryption practices, incident notification terms and use of subprocessors. A vendor whose assessment is overdue is treated as having no mitigation credit when residual risk is calculated.

## 4. Contract terms

Contracts must require breach notification within 72 hours, the right to audit, disclosure of subprocessors, and return or deletion of data at termination.

## 5. Findings

Vendor findings are tracked with severity and due dates. Findings rated HIGH or CRITICAL that remain open past their due date are escalated to the Security Manager.
